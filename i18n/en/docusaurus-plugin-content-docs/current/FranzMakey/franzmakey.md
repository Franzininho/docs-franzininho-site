FRANZmakey is a kit that works similarly to the Makey Makey, but at a much more accessible price and made in Brazil. The goal is to enable interaction with a computer through unusual objects — fruits, vegetables, and more — stimulating creativity and computational thinking through hands-on Scratch programming.

![FranzMakey Kit](img/kit-FranzMakey.jpeg)

## What can I do with the FRANZmakey?

Put your creativity to work: build a controller for Scratch games, animate Scratch characters by touching fruit, create stories, make music with vegetables, and more.

## What will I learn?

Computational thinking, creativity, visual block programming (Scratch), and human-computer interaction concepts.

![FranzMakey with fruit](img/kit-franzmakey-frutas.JPG)

## What is included in the kit

- 1 FRANZmakey board
- 1 USB cable (2 meters)
- 5 alligator clip cables
- Instruction manual
- Project examples
