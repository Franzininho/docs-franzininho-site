## What is the Badge?

The Learn to Solder badge was developed for soldering workshops where participants take their first steps with soldering tools and gain knowledge of electronic components and circuit boards.

With just a few components, this badge lets anyone learn to solder and have fun.

![Badge assembly](img/project-badge-aprenda-soldar-00.jpeg)

The circuit chosen for this badge is the classic astable multivibrator using transistors. After soldering, the robot's eyes blink, and the board is ready to use as a necklace or keychain.

During the workshop, participants learn to use a soldering iron and understand the process of soldering PTH components onto a board.

Learn to Solder badge workshops are aimed at adults and teenagers interested in learning about electronic soldering. A session lasts about 1 hour, depending on group size.
